## Direction

- Describe the product-specific hierarchy, layout, typography and interaction intent.
- Respect the established design system and explain changes by user need.
- Identify evidence for testimonials, statistics, security and performance claims; remove unsupported claims.
- Do not equate “no gradients” or “looks human” with usability.
